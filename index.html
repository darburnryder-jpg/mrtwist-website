Common Questions

Before you book.

What areas do you perform in?+
All of Victoria — every Melbourne suburb, plus regional centres and country towns. Based in Mount Waverley. Travel outside metro Melbourne can be arranged for the right event.
Do you perform for adults or only for kids?+
Both. The show is tailored to land equally well with kids, families and adult audiences — from corporate events and weddings through to birthday parties and school events.
How far in advance should I book?+
The earlier the better, especially for weekends and October (Halloween) or November-December (end-of-year events). Last-minute bookings are often possible mid-week — call to check.
Do you bring live animals?+
Yes — live dove illusions are a feature of the stage show. All animals are cared for to the highest standard and are experienced performers.
What's included in the stage show?+
The 30-minute magic show includes grand illusions, live doves, fire magic, comedy ventriloquism with a realistic puppet, and audience participation throughout. Show length and content tailored to your event.
