FAQ

Common questions

What kind of events do you perform at?+
Kids birthday parties, corporate events, weddings, Halloween shows, gothic and themed parties, community festivals, mall entertainment, and adult private events. Amazing Mr Twist entertains any age group with tailored performances.
Do you use real animals in your shows?+
Yes — real live doves feature in the stage magic show, plus a rabbit for classic magic performances. All animals are trained and cared for professionally.
What's the difference between the magic show and roving magic?+
The magic show is a 30-minute seated stage show with illusions, fire magic, doves, comedy ventriloquism and audience participation. Roving close-up magic is mingling entertainment for guests during cocktails or dinner — card magic, rope tricks, money magic and interactive comedy.
Do you travel outside Melbourne?+
Yes — based in Mount Waverley, servicing all of Melbourne and regional Victoria. Travel fees may apply for locations far from the metro area. Get in touch for a custom quote.
How far in advance should I book?+
For popular dates (school holidays, October for Halloween, November-December for end-of-year events), book 4-8 weeks ahead. Last-minute bookings often possible mid-week — call to check availability.
