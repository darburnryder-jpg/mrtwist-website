FAQ

Common adult event questions

Is the magic actually good, or is this a kids show for adults?+
Real sleight-of-hand and mentalism. Guests can inspect props, choose cards, borrow rings — nothing is gimmick-heavy. The comedy is adult-appropriate, the pacing is different from a kids show. Two decades of professional performance experience across corporate and adult events.
Can you tailor the character to our theme?+
Yes — Captain Twist pirate character, gothic horror, classic top hat magician, or custom character work for corporate themes. Call to discuss your event vision.
Do you perform at licensed venues?+
Yes — full public liability insurance covers licensed venues, function centres, hotels, nightclubs and corporate offices. Insurance certificate available on request.
How does roving magic work at a cocktail party?+
I move through the room from group to group, performing 3-5 minute close-up sets for each cluster of guests. Everyone gets multiple moments during the hour. Works brilliantly for cocktail hours, drinks receptions and networking events.
Can you MC or host as well as perform?+
Yes — MC/host duties can be added to any stage show booking. Discuss requirements when booking.
