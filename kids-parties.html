FAQ

Common kids party questions

What age range works best?+
The show is tailored to the age group — ideal for 3-12 year olds. For younger kids (3-5) the show is more visual and silly; for older kids (7-12) more mystery and interactive challenges. Just let me know the age range when you book.
How much space do we need?+
Roughly a 3x3 metre performance area, plus space for kids to sit in front. Most living rooms, backyards, halls and party rooms work fine.
Do you supply everything?+
Yes — all magic props, sound, backdrop and animals. All you need is the space and the kids.
Can you do the balloons instead of magic?+
Yes — balloon twisting only bookings are available on request. Or combine the show with balloons as Package C for the full experience.
Do you charge travel fees?+
Metro Melbourne generally no. Regional Victoria or long-distance travel may add a small travel fee — always quoted upfront so there are no surprises.
